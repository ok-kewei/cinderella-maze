Cinderella's Midnight Maze

How to play

The household

  • CinderellaThat's you. Clear the halls of every sparkle.
  • The StepmotherComes straight for you, and hurries when few pearls remain. Sometimes she shouts for the family when she spots you down a hallway, and they all speed up. Watch out too: the family can slip on her soap buckets.
  • GriseldaAims four tiles ahead of you to cut you off. Sometimes she stops to argue with Petunia when they bump into each other, or joins the singing lesson on a squeaky flute.
  • PetuniaWorks off her mother's position to trap you from the other side, and argues right back. Sometimes she stops for a singing lesson: Soot covers his ears and freezes, and you slow down if you're close to her.
  • SootPounces from afar, then slinks back to his corner when he gets close.

Magic & points

  • Magic twirlPress Space (or the ✦ button on the pad) to twirl with a burst of speed. While you twirl, the family can't catch you. The wand at the top refills before you can twirl again.
  • Glass slipperThe Fairy Godmother turns the whole family into mice for a few seconds. Catch them.
  • Enchanted mouseCaught mice head home as a pair of eyes and change back.
  • Bonus prizesAppear below the family's house twice a level. Each level has its own, from the pumpkin to the royal crown, and the strip under the maze shows them all.
  • Prince CharmingCatch all four mice from one glass slipper and he waltzes with Cinderella for a moment. Worth a 3,000 bonus.
  • Midnight rushNow and then the palace clock strikes twelve. While the 12 chimes ring, every pearl is worth double.
  • The candles go outNow and then the palace goes dark for a few seconds. You only see the glow around Cinderella and the family's eyes.
  • The Grand DukeSometimes he walks the halls with the slipper on its cushion. He never hurts you, but you can't get past him.
  • Fairy GodmotherSometimes she pops in and turns one of the family into a pumpkin for a few seconds. A pumpkin can't move or catch you.
  • BarleySometimes the old dog bounds in and chases Soot off to his corner. He never bothers you.
  • Ball of yarnNow and then a ball of yarn turns up. Pick it up and it rolls away, and Soot forgets all about you to chase it.
  • The beadsThe stepsisters' string of beads is worth a lot, but take it and Petunia wants them back: she comes after you, furious and faster, for a while.
  • The attic keySometimes the Stepmother locks a gate across a hallway. Find the key to open it again, for a bonus.
  • Pip and CrumbNow and then one of them scurries through the halls. Catch Pip and he sweeps up every pearl in the hallways around you. Catch Crumb and he ties Soot up for 5 seconds.
  • Dropped choresThe Stepmother leaves buckets, brooms and laundry behind her. Step on one and you slow down for a moment.
